In freedom from serious crime Canada enjoys a good name and reputation 
which is in striking contrast to that of its great neighbor, the United States.

fully refrain from policies and actions which would in the slightest degree | 
lower the standard so long maintained in this Dominion. A word of warning 
is in order because signs are not lacking of a tendency to grow somewhat lax 
in the matter of law enforcement. °

respects the law has broken down. So serious has the situation become that | 
responsible officials and nation-wide organizations, including the legal profes- | 
fion, are alarmed, and are looking about for some effective means of check- |
