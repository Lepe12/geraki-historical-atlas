BOOK’S INFOS

Title: The Mediterranean Hawk (Eleonora’s Falcon)
Written by: Leonidas G. Gourgourinis
Published by: Arteon Publications
Year of Publication: 2020

The book (first volume of a trilogy), written by Leonidas G. Gourgourinis, is an historical fiction novel based on the true story and life of the Greek pirate, rebel and spy Petros Lantzas who lived in late 16th and early 17th century.
