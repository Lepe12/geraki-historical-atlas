ous pastime, not only for the cyclist, 
but for motor traffic. A big bus 
overturned near VVaverley the other 
(my when the driver tried to dodge 
two girl bicyclists, and a number "of 
passengers were hurt. By good for¬ 
tune there were no fatalities. The 
girls, were travelling in the middlfe of 
the road and weaving from side to 
side. , 

If cyclists desire to use the high¬ 
ways, they should obey the rules of 
the road and stick close to the right- 
hand side of the pavement. Provision 
should be made in post-war highway 
building for a yard strip for cyclists
