Argentiera is a place that must be experienced. A simple description cannot fully convey the secrets and that sensation of complete living that one feels in this small but kaleidoscopic corner of earth.

Cellar Tour

A strikingly picturesque spot, infused with beauty, but one of rigorous work as well

From the panoramic vantage-point of our cellar terrace, you can admire the outlines of the islands of the Tuscan archipelago and appreciate the cellar’s unique architecture, built on the model of classic 16th-century Tuscan fortresses.
 

- Cellar tour
- Tasting in our Enoteca
- 1 hour and 30 minutes

Vineyard Experience

A majestic natural amphitheater facing the sea, captivates the senses of those who go down and climb up

In the midst of the vine-rows of Argentiera, under the spell of the genius loci, where sea breezes and the pungent sent of the Mediterranean scrub meet and dance together
 

- Tour the cellar and the Argentiera vineyard
- Tasting in reserved room
- 2 hours
