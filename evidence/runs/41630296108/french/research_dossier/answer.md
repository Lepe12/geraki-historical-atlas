# Ευρήματα από τις πηγές

What do contemporary travellers and archival sources state about merchant anchorage and drinking-water supply on Kimolos (Argentiera)? Keep publication dates separate from event dates. Do not infer piracy from the presence of ships. Cite actual newly acquired passages and report unread sources, inaccessible repositories and historical-verification gaps.
