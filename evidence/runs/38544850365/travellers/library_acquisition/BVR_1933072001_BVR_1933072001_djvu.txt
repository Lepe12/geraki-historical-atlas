Extermination Of Weeds 


Summer Plowing With Double 
Strike-Out Method Most Effective 
Summer plowing is an effective 

method of killing such weeds as sow 
thistle and twitch grass. Often, how- 
ever, the strike-out leaves a strip of 
undisturbed weeds which again pol- 
lute the field. The double strike-out 
should be employed where possible, 
that is, first make a shallow dead 
furrow, then turn two light furrows 
back and complete the strike-out 
with two heavier furrows. This up- 
roots all the soil and leaves no undis- 
turbed strips on which the weeds may. 
continue to thrive.—Farmer’s Advo- 
cate.
