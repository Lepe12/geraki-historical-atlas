Robert Rose, aged 71, resident of 
Milo and well known oldtime cow¬ 
boy, died suddenly in the Vulcan 
hospital on Saturday* Oct. 27. 

Born in Scotland he came to 
Alberts before the" turn of 'the 
century and homesteaded four 
miles south of Milo. He later 
worked with the C.C. Co., and with 
the old “O" Ranch at Queenstown 
and then, for 35 years, he rode 
tor Douglas Hardwick’s Lazy H 
Ranch until its breakup in 1943, 
when he moved to Milo. 

A well known cowpuncher of 
the plains country, “Rosy," as he 
was known to all, returned to 
England when World War I start¬ 
ed and enlisted in -the Royal Sus¬ 
sex Regiment, transferring a few 
months later to the 1st Div. Cana¬ 
dian Corps Cyclists. He served in 
France and Belgium until the 
Armistice,’ receiving his discharge 
in May 1919. Returning to Canada, 
he rcusmed his job at the Hard-

Machine uses vertical steel molds 
to form the pipe, the inside mold 
remaining stationary, while the 
outside mold rotates. The con¬ 
crete feeds slowly into the molds 
nnd is tamped constantly by high 
speed tampers. 

The. reinforcing for a pipe Is 
supplied to the plant as steel 
mesh in large rolls, which is 
passed through rollers to form it 
to the correct curvature of the 
pipe being manufactured. It is 
then cut and the ends welded to¬ 
gether to form a cage to drop into 
the pipe molds. 

From the pipe , machine the 
newly made pipes with the .inner 
form removed, are carried by lift 
truck to the curling kilns, of 
w hich there are four. Here the 
outside form is removed and the 
pipe inspected for any imperfec¬ 
tion. The pipes are then steam 
cured and afterwards moved to 
the stock piles in the yard.

District News 


The Communications Department of the County 
Civil Defence Organization needs Radio operators. 

To fill this need, a Radio Course, as laid out by 
the Department of Transport, will be conducted. 

This course will commence on Wednfesday, Nov. 
14, at 7.30 p.m. and continue every Wednesday at the 
same time. 

At the completion of this course candidates will 
be examined by the Department Radio Inspector and 
if successful will be issued with a special Radio 
Operators Certificate for Civil Defence operation. 

Any man or woman who would like to take this 
course please contact either Wm. Shields or D. L. Wil¬ 
son or report on Wednesday, Nov. 14, at the Civil 
Defence Headquarters in the cottage school, Vulcan.

NOVEMBER 12-17 IS F.UA. WEEK 

The co-operation of Alberta farmers will deter¬ 
mine the success of this very important Farmers’ 
Union membership drive. The issue is squarely up to 
the farmers to take this opportunity to build a strong 
Farmer’s Union. * 

U.F.A. Co-op, organized by farmers to serve 
farmers in the farm supply field, is keenly interested 
in the (Development of a strong Farmers’ organization. 
To get a fair deal for agriculture it is essential to have 
a farm organization with membership representing 
the majority of the Farmers of Alberta. 

U.F.A. Co-op strongly urges all farmers and par¬ 
ticularly its members to take an active part in F.U.A. 
Week, /oin the Farmers’ Union of Alberta. Get your 
neighbors to join.
