## claim-travellers-event-0003
Status: **UNRESOLVED**
Research Value: **100/100**

### Preserved claim/evidence text
According to Rukšans (2010), on eastern Crete, in the Lassithi plain, Crocus 
tournefortii J.Gay sometimes hybridises with Crocus laevigatus Bory & Chaub. Several 
specimens demonstrating intermediate characteristics between the two taxa, in fila- 
ment length and pubescence, were found on Kimolos. To our knowledge, this is the 
first time that such a hybridisation event has been reported outside the Cretan area.
