FOOTPRINTS IN THE SAND 
RULING DYNASTIES OF TH MAGHREB FROM THE 7™ TO 1774 CENTURIES 


Footprints in the Sand (740pp) is the culmination of over fifteen years of research by Dr. 
Brian A. Smith concerning the Maghreb, specifically the al-Hasani period of Morocco, 
the rule of the Abd al-Wady from Tlemcen, the Hafsid dynasty of Tunis and the al-Fihri 
family of Egypt and al-Andalus. While being a story centered on Jan Jansen van Haarlem, 
a Dutch privateer from the Netherlands at the end of the sixteenth century (and the 
subject of an upcoming Dutch Public Television 4-hour biography), it also provides a 
concise and relatively complete listing and accomplishments of the afore-named 
dynasties complete with full references. Jan Jansen worked with, and for, the sultan of 
Morocco and married a daughter of the Sultan. Dr. Smith traces the ancestry of Morjana 
bint Abd-er-Rahman al-Shaabi through the al-Hasani, Abd al-Wady, and Fihrid dynasties. 
The ancestry is complimented by forays into the role of the marabouts in Moroccan 
politics, the conquering of the Songhai Empire in 1590 by the most illustrious al-Hasani 
ruler, al-Mansur (1549-1603), and more. Contains many charts, illustrations, and maps. 


Part One covers General History; Part 2 covers the life of Jan Jansen van Haarlem; Part 3 
covers the afore-named dynasties; and Part 4 contains seven Appendices.
