SIDEWALKS AND FIREWALLS 


With good weather and plenty of 
material, work on the cement side- 
walks throughout the business por- 
tion of the town is making big head- 
way. Already Baxter Bros., the 
contractors, have finished the west 
side of Second Street with the excep- 
tion of the King Edward Hotel 
Block, and have the sidewalk laid 
down most of the way on the east 
side of the street. 

Mr Frank Bond, contractor for 
the two firewalls to be built on the 
west side of Second Street has al- 
‘ready finished the excavatiin on the 
one between Mr E, Brown's 
store and the Opera House. The 
material taken out is being used to 
grade up the streets. Several cars 
of bricks and other material for the 
erection of the walls have arrived. 

Tenders for the $10,000 worth of 

! plank sidewalks are to be in to the 
Town Secretary by Monday, Sep- 
tember 16, and work will be rushed 
ahead as much as possible once the 
contract is let.
