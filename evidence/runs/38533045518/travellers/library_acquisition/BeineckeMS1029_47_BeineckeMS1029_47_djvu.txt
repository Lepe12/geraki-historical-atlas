New Haven, CT, Yale University, Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library, Beinecke MS 1029 


Description: ff. 113 - Binding ca. 1500??: blind-tooled leather over wooden boards. Spine with four raised bands. On the 
front cover a parchment label with the sixteenth century inscription in Northern Gothica Textualis Formata "Cronicques / 
abreigiés". - Tajan sale, 15 June 1995, lot 6; acquired by Bernard M. Rosenthal (MS 187 in his collection); purchased from 
him in 2003 on the Edwin J. Beinecke Fund. 

Notes:-Manuscript on paper of 1) Noël de Fribois (d. 1467/1468), Abrégé des croniques de France, presented to King 
Charles VII of France in 1459. 2) Simon Gréban (d. ca. 1473), Epitaph for King Charles VII (1461). 3) Moral aphorisms in 
the form of distichs. 4) Moral aphorisms in French after the manner of the Disticha Catonis, perhaps by the same author as 
art. 3. 5) Catalogue of the library of Jaspar Scaeck, apparently a lawyer in northern France (Lille?). The 57 books, listed 
without a clear order, are almost all in French and were printed between 1534 and 1595, with one book dated 1495; they 
mostly were produced in Paris, Lyons, Douai and Antwerp. For each the owner gives a full transcription of the title page, 
reproducing its layout (exceptionnally also the colophon); for the last two items also a note on the binding.
