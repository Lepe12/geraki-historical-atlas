Big Boys Interview by Chris Cummings 

The Big Boys have become one of the most 
popular skate bands around today. I talked 
with Tim Kerr recently about the band. 

Chris-First of all whats the current line up? 

Randy Turner, Chris Gates, Rey Washam.and 
me, Tim Kerr. 

Chris-When were the Big Boys formed ? 

In Austin, Texas in 1977. We got 
together once to play and all this 
stuff has been amazing. 

Chris-What do you mean ? 

I mean we are serious about what we are 
doing but its not like were gonna do 
this and well get a big record deal. 

Chris-What do you think of the scene in Texas? 

In Texas the punk/hardcore scene is more 
attitude than 1,2,3, kind of music 

Chris-Have you always played punk ? 

Yeah,weve always stayed on the wilder 
side of music. 

Chris-What are your musical influences ? Punk, Jazz, Funk, heavy metal, etc ... . 

Chris-How do you feel about your shows? Our shows are pretty much a free for 
all. If you know the songs you can get upland sing. As soon as the first 
chords of our songs start the stage usually starts filling up.
