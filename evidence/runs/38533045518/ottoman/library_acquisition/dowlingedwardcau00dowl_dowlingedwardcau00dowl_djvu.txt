Gentlemen:  - 


I  am  in  receipt  of  yours  of  the  4th  instant  in  relation  to 
Mr.  Ed.  Fro s sard. 


In  reply  I  am  sorry  to  say  that  it  is  impossible  at  the  present 
time  to  say  when  Mr.  Frossard  will  be  in  a  position  to  pay  your  bill. 

I  have  received  advices  from  him,  the  latest  of  which  informs 
me  that  he  has  only  just  been  able  to  obtain  employment,  his  illness 
having  been  so  severe  as  to  prevent  him  heretofore.  He  has  informed  me 


that  ne  expects  to  start  paying  his  debts  on  the  first  of  January  next; 
further  than  that  I  have  no  knowledge  of  his  affairs. 

Your  statement  that  the  goods  for  which  he  owes  you  were  entrust¬ 
ed  to  him  as  agent  and  not  sold  to  him,  I  must  on  his  behalf  deny. 

Yours  very  truly,
