New York did not exist for more than thirty years after the ancestors of the Smith family 
arrived in this part of the New World. For them it was: 


NEW AMSTERDAM 


in the DUTCH PROVINCE of NEW NETHERLAND 


Photography had yet to be invented rather there were court records, 
drawings, painting, etchings and even a bit of plagiarism! 


Proceedings of the Court in New Amsterdam in which some of GRIETJE REYNIER’S less- 


savory quirks are highlighted.

relates the time when she allegedly 
lifted her skirts, slapped her exposed 
bottom and yelled what was basically 
the Dutch equivalent of 


“Kiss my a**!”

of the harbor who are calling her a 


“2 pound butter whore”

salt, or at least unsalted butter. They were 
part of a conspiracy to gain the land GRIETJE
