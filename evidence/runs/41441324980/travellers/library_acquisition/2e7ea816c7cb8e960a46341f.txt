    All Episodes                             

                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 1
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Explorer
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Commander Cottontail needs his Explorer suit cleaned, Milo, Lofty and Lark are mesmerized listening to his exciting adventures. With Suds' help, they transport to the jungle to embark on an exciting treasure hunt.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 2
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the News Reporter
                                                
                                                

                                                    News reporter Harriet excites Milo, Lofty and Lark with her chase stories. When Milo volunteers to help Harriet find a front-page headline for The Gazette, the gang must find news that's exciting, surprising and dramatic!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 3
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Dancer
                                                
                                                

                                                    Inspired by dancers Eleanor and Mycroft, Milo, Lofty and Lark are transported to the Dance Theatre stage. But when they have to step in to perform in the show, they must overcome their stage fright if they want to deliver a Spectacular Dance!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 4
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Doctor
                                                
                                                

                                                    A tired Doctor Diamond needs her uniform cleaned, so she visits Scrubby's. When Milo, Lofty and Lark end up helping her in her busy hospital ward, she takes a well-deserved nap. The trio soon realizes that making patients feel better is hard work.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 5
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Chef
                                                
                                                

                                                    Chef Pierre's stories inspire Milo, Lofty and Lark to want to work in his world-famous restaurant. After serving some crazy culinary disasters, will they be able to remember the secret of captivating cooking to prepare a dish fit for a queen?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 6
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Cruise Captain
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark would love to sail the world like Captain Stone visiting loads of fascinating places, so Suds transports them aboard his luxurious cruise ship. But when Milo takes the helm, he discovers it's not all plain sailing.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 7
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Farmer
                                                
                                                

                                                    When muddy farmer Tilly visits Scrubby's, Milo, Lofty and Lark think it would be amazing to be on the farm with the animals. But if left in charge, their fun soon turns to chaos. Will they remember the Countryside Code in time to restore order?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 8
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Mountaineer
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark are delighted by Commander Cottontail's stories of daring expeditions, so Suds transports our intrepid trio to climb Mount Neverest! At first it's great fun, but they must remember to respect the mountain to reach the summit.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 9
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Footballer
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark are very excited when footballer Charlie Cheetah needs his kit cleaned for the big Cup Final. But when they end up playing with the first team, their opponents soon take the lead! Will they be able to turn the game around by playing to their strengths?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 10
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Postal Worker
                                                
                                                

                                                    Come rain or shine, Postman Raymond always delivers the town's mail and Milo, Lofty and Lark are delighted to help with such important work. But with the storm brewing, will they be able to provide a first-class service?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 11
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Mechanic
                                                
                                                

                                                    Mechanic Dave's job of fixing lots of different vehicles sounds like great fun, so when Milo, Lofty and Lark join him in his garage, they're eager to start fixing things! Let's hope they find the right parts to get things running for their customers.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 12
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Hairdresser
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Suds transports Milo, Lofty and Lark to Stevie Snipz's super-cool hair salon, they get to work styling their demanding clients and waiting for their mysterious VIP to arrive. Despite some hair-raising confusion, will everyone be delighted with their new look?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 13
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Botanist
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Heidi Hop arrives at Scrubby's to get her muddy outfit cleaned, Milo, Lofty and Lark feel inspired to embark on a botanical adventure and discover the rarest plant of all: one that's never been seen before!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 14
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Supermarket Worker
                                                
                                                

                                                    Leonard the Lizard loves his job at the supermarket and Milo, Lofty and Lark are delighted to work in the deli, checkout and stacking shelves. But when a strange squeak makes all the customers run away, will our trio be able to make it a happy shopping place again?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 15
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Artist
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark love painting and when they meet professional artist Shona in her studio, they're eager to express their artistic side. But will the pressure of revealing a real portrait at Shona's Grand Exhibition be too much?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 16
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Police Officer
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo, Lofty and Lark join the police force to help Officer Rhino, they soon find themselves on the trail of a food thief! The crafty Mr. Croc seems like a likely suspect, but our trio soon discovers that being responsible for law enforcement is a complicated matter.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 17
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Tour Guide
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo, Lofty and Lark become tour guides for a day at the Royal Castle, they hope to meet Queen Mabel herself! But after discovering secret underground tunnels, the group of tourists gets much more than they expected.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 18
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Photographer
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark are eager to help flamboyant photographer Vincent Verona capture the centerpiece image of his new exhibition: a very rare bird, the elusive Pygmy. But will our trio have the patience needed to take the perfect photo?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 19
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Librarian
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark love reading books and comics, so when they become librarians they're eager to help everyone find a book they'll love, even people who think there's no book for them, because there are books about absolutely everything!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 20
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Ice Skater
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Dame Dolores has a double booking for the Dancing On Skates final and performing at the opera, Milo, Lofty and Lark take to the ice in her place. But will they master the basics in time to beat their competition, the crafty Mr. Croc and his army of bees?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 21
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Musician
                                                
                                                

                                                    Musicians Milo, Lofty and Lark are excited about playing at the village festival and replacing Dave and the Dodgers with their own band. But writing their own song is harder than it seems. If they sing about what they know, will the audience go along with it?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 22
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Theatre Actor
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark think it will be great fun to take part in the Dubois' play, but it looks like their performance will be a flop. But the show must go on and once our trusty trio decides to make it up as they go along, will they be able to win over the...                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 23
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Judge
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Judge Jackson has to referee a football match, Milo, Lofty and Lark are left in charge of court proceedings. But to make any judgment they need to have all the facts - and sometimes things aren't exactly as they seem. Will justice be served?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 24
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Tennis Player
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo, Lofty and Lark take part in the Milotown Tennis Championship, it's ball boy Lofty who demonstrates better teamwork off the court. Milo and Lark, doubles partners, will remember that the team that plays together, wins together, and take home the trophy!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 25
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Race Car Driver
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark are very excited to discover the exciting world of Grand Prix racing, especially when they compete as part of Tania Turbo's team! But speed alone won't be enough to beat their rival, the crafty Mr. Crocodile: they'll need strategy!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 26
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Firefighter
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo, Lofty and Lark become heroic firefighters so Chief Tyrone can be honored at the palace, our brave trio has little time to train and needs to learn fast. But if they stay calm and communicate, they'll be able to save the day!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 27
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Archaeologist
                                                
                                                

                                                    When our intrepid trio sets off in search of the lost city of Catlantis and its famous fountains, all they discover are pebbles! But the smallest clues often lead to the greatest discoveries... so who knows where their adventure will lead!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 28
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Delivery Driver
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark know how important delivery drivers are to many people, so they're polite and kind. But trying to please everyone leaves one customer disappointed. Will the three friends be able to deliver exactly what the customer ordered?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 29
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Fashion Designer
                                                
                                                

                                                    House of Stevie's new fashion collection needs a fabulous centerpiece for its runway show. But it's up to Milo, Lofty and Lark to design, make and model their creations. It's hard, hot and thirsty work, but will their mistakes lead to success on the catwalk?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 30
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Dentist
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo, Lofty and Lark take care of Horace's dental practice, they love making sure no one leaves without a dazzling smile. Even the crafty Mr. Crocodile learns that going to the dentist is a very good thing, especially when there are stickers!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 31
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Astronaut
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo, Lofty and Lark report to Mission Control, they're soon on their way to the Moon to save Mila's precious space strawberries. But with so much to discover and explore in outer space, will they manage to complete their mission?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 32
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Clown
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark are delighted to enter the Big Top to entertain the audience, but poor Milo gets upset when he thinks everyone is laughing at him. Horace has to remind them that when the audience laughs with you, it's the best sound in the world!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 33
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Secret Agent
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo, Lofty and Lark are recruited to protect the Royal Cookbook from the crafty Mr. Croc, they end up trying to save Agent Stone too! Our sharp-suited trio will need all their high-tech gadgets and look beyond the obvious to rescue both.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 34
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Taxi Driver
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo and his friends arrive at work at Turbo Taxis, they have to pick up Dame Delores for her big concert that evening. But as they pick up more customers along the way, will they manage to get everyone to their destination, with or without GPS?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 35
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Factory Worker
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo and his friends are making jam at Farmer Tilly's factory, but they spend too much time eating the fruit. They speed up the machine to make up for lost time, but end up in a sticky situation. Will they be ready for the harvest and even create a new recipe?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 36
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Rescue Pilot
                                                
                                                

                                                    Despite the excitement of piloting a helicopter, will Milo, Lofty and Lark be able to stay calm in the face of danger and work as a team to respond to distress calls? Will our brave heroes be able to save the day when they face not one, but TWO rescue missions?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 37
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Athlete
                                                
                                                

                                                    With Charlie's Rockets team unexpectedly injured, it's up to Milo, Lofty and Lark to compete against Crafty Mr Croc in The Milotown Epic Athletics. But to lift the trophy as true athletes, they must remember to focus... despite the distractions!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 38
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Fisherman
                                                
                                                

                                                    Chef Pierre invites our intrepid trio out to sea to fish for his restaurant. But after catching more rubbish than fish, saving an entangled whale and cleaning up their patch of ocean, will Milo and his friends manage to catch something for the menu?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 39
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Author
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo, Lofty and Lark are invited to write a new story for the Milotown Book Festival, they worry they don't have good ideas. But when they write about their day - a story of superheroes, adventures, emotions and... chickens - they receive very favorable reviews!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 40
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Builder
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo and his friends want to help Otto, but when they accept two construction jobs, they soon run out of time and materials. But a good builder never leaves a job unfinished, so will our trusty trio be able to use their strengths and keep everyone happy?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 41
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Baker
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Rodney suddenly has to swap his baker's hat for a police cap, Milo, Lofty and Lark take his place to compete in the Bake-A-Thon Tent. But if they have any hope of winning the coveted Golden Spoon, they'll need to follow Rodney's recipe to the letter!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 42
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Film Director
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo and his friends are delighted to help Vincent Verona direct his new superhero movie. Great directors always stay true to the script, but the three friends listen to too many ideas and make too many changes, so the movie is almost a disaster!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 43
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Gardener
                                                
                                                

                                                    Mrs. Sweeper rushes off to prune for Dame Delores, so Milo, Lofty and Lark have to prepare the park for a big competition. But they forget to follow the plan and chaos ensues. Will they be able to make a new plan, stick to it and create a perfect park?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 44
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Lifeguard
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo, Lofty and Lark work hard to watch over the crowded beach and keep everyone safe. They do a good job, but the crafty Mr. Croc wants the beach all to himself and hatches a terrifying plan. Milo and his friends will need to stay alert to restore order.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 45
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Paleontologist
                                                
                                                

                                                    When our brave trio joins Heidi to dig up dinosaur bones, they don't know how they fit together. But those bones can build a picture of the past and soon they're on the right track - plus, Lofty sniffs out a very important discovery!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 46
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Veterinarian
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Milo, Lofty and Lark help Doc Diamond care for some sick animals at Farmer Tilly's farm, they struggle to figure out what's wrong with them. The friends have to remember that sometimes eyes and ears are a veterinarian's most useful healing equipment.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 47
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Robotics Engineer
                                                
                                                

                                                    Mila is full of brilliant ideas and always says a robot is only as good as its programming. But when our trusty trio builds a robot to keep birds away from Farmer Tilly's fields, they end up scaring the whole town! Time to reprogram it!                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 48
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Monarch
                                                
                                                

                                                    A delighted Milo replaces Queen Mabel and becomes King for a day to carry out royal duties in Milotown. But he learns that it takes more than a crown to be a monarch and that helping his subjects is much more important than any title or royal galas.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 49
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Teacher
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo and his friends are delighted to teach science, music and art to Miss Stern's evening class students, but unfortunately not everyone responds to their teaching methods. The best way to teach is to make sure everyone has fun, but how will they do it?                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 50
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Florist
                                                
                                                

                                                    Milo and his friends have to be creative when they're left in charge of Blossoms and Blooms flower shop. Everyone wants flowers and soon they have none left for a VIP exhibition. If they think about the right plants for each occasion, everything will be fine.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 51
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Cleaner
                                                
                                                

                                                    When Mrs. Sweeper has to deal with an emergency with pickled onions, Milo and his friends want to thoroughly clean Queen Mabel's dusty palace. They think vacuum Vic might be a quick solution, but sometimes old-fashioned elbow grease gets the best results.                                                
                                            
                                        
                                    
                                                                    

                                        
                                            

                                                                                            
                                            
                                            

                                                

                                                                                                            Episode 52
                                                                                                    
                                                
                                                Milo the Decorator
                                                
                                                

                                                    Otto is required elsewhere for a royal appointment, so Milo and his friends get to work painting Dame Delores' ballroom. But a mix of party food and a hungry Bruno causes chaos. How will they exceed Otto's client expectations and achieve a five-star review?
