La Commissione di Governo del Piemonte 
Reorganization of the National Senate under the French Republic 


Date: 17 July 1800 (28 Messidor, Year VIII of the French Republic) 


This is one of the most important documents for your research because it records the 
restructuring of the Piedmontese judicial system under the French administration after 
Napoleon’s victory at Marengo. 


Most importantly, it shows that Paoletti was still active in high public office in July 1800, 
serving as a substitute prosecutor within the National Senate.

2. English Translation 


The Commission of Government of Piedmont reorganizes the newly created National Senate. 
The decree establishes: 


President 

Vice Presidents 
Senators 

Attorney General 

Fiscal Attorney General 
Advocate for the Poor 
Secretaries 

Substitute officials 


Among the substitute officials attached to the office of the Procuratore de’ Poveri 
(Advocate/Prosecutor for the Poor) are: 


Paoletti 
Grisiana 
Demaria 


The Secretary of State for Internal Affairs is ordered to implement the decree. 


3. Summary 


This decree reorganizes the judicial system of Piedmont after the French takeover. 
It establishes the personnel of the: 

Senato Nazionale 

(National Senate) 

on 17 July 1800. 


The document lists dozens of officials and judicial officers. 


Most importantly for your research: 


Paoletti appears among the officially appointed substitute officers attached to the 
Procuratore dei Poveri. 


4. Significance 


A. Identification of Paoletti 


The document identifies: 

Paoletti 

Sostituto 

within the National Senate structure. 

Although no first name is given, the timing is extremely significant. 
Only two months later, on 16 September 1800, you found: 

Notaio Chiaffredo Paoletti 

appointed Vice Inspector of prisons and judicial institutions. 


This makes it highly probable that the Paoletti listed here is the same Chiaffredo Paoletti. 


B. Career Continuity 
This document demonstrates that the Paoletti family successfully navigated the transition from: 


e Kingdom of Sardinia administration 
to 
e French Republican administration. 


Many officials lost office after the French occupation. 


Paoletti appears instead to have remained within the judicial bureaucracy. 


C. Relationship with Grisiana and Demaria 


Notice that the three substitute officers are: 


e Paoletti 
e Grisiana 
e Demaria 


Two months later you found another decree: 


e Grisiana appointed to a senior charitable office. 
e Demaria pensioned. 
e Chiaffredo Paoletti promoted to Vice Inspector. 


The same three names reappear together. 


This suggests they belonged to the same administrative circle.

5. Timeline Impact 


The Paoletti chronology now becomes: 


Date Event 

1749 Licentiate in Law (Gaspare Angelo Paoletti) 
1750 Doctor of Law 

1751 College of Jurists 

1766 Avvocato Generale of the Senate of Piedmont 
1774 Extraordinary Judge 

1775 Ordinary Judge 

1782 Senatorial remuneration granted 


17 Jul 1800 Paoletti listed as Substitute Officer of the National Senate 


16 Sep 1800 Chiaffredo Paoletti appointed Vice Inspector of prisons 


6. Historical Importance 


This document captures a unique moment immediately after the French conquest of Piedmont. 
It shows: 


the abolition or restructuring of the old Senate, 

creation of a National Senate, 

appointment of new judicial officers, 

survival of several established legal families within the new regime. 


For your family research, the most valuable point is that it places Paoletti inside the governing 
judicial structure of Piedmont in July 1800, only weeks before the decree naming Chiaffredo 
Paoletti personally. This strengthens the evidence that Chiaffredo was an important legal official 
during the Napoleonic transition in Piedmont.
