S10.2 VENUS DE MILO

DETAILS
Museum Collection Musée du Louvre, Paris
Catalogue No. Louvre Ma399
Title Venus de Milo
Type Statue
Material Marble
Height 2.02 metres
Context Melos
Original / Copy Greek original
Style Hellenistic
Date ca 130 - 100 B.C.
Period Late Hellenistic

DESCRIPTION

The goddess Aphrodite stands partially disrobed. Despite being a Greek statue from the Aegean island of Melos this celebrated artwork is commonly known as "Venus".
