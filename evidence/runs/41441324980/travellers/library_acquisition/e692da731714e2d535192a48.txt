The island of dreamlike beaches


Milos is an island in the southwestern Cyclades, located roughly midway between Piraeus and Crete, and is considered one of the most impressive island destinations in the Aegean Sea. Its name is renowned, thanks to the striking statue of Aphrodite unearthed here, now exhibited at the Louvre Museum in Paris.

Milos is also famed for its numerous, idyllic beaches. As a volcanic island, it boasts striking geological formations, with white, red, yellow, and black hued rocks, white sandy shores, and rocks sculpted by time and the sea.

Its volcanic past is etched into the dramatic caves and unique coastal formations, exemplified by Kleftiko beach, where the cluster of sheer white volcanic rocks is breathtaking. The island's hot springs have been noted since antiquity and are referenced by Hippocrates.

Milos boasts significant archaeological sites and museums, with the Milos Mining Museum highlighting its long and storied mining history.

With many picturesque hamlets and over 70 impressive beaches, rich history and local products, Milos will undoubtedly remain unforgettable.



Highlights
Volcanic Beauty: Dramatic landscapes and colorful rock formations.

Unique Beaches: Famous spots like Sarakiniko and Kleftiko.

Seaside Villages: Charming fishing villages along the coast.
