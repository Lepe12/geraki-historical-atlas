Welcome to the OED AI Assistant
                
            
            

                

                    

                        
The AI Search Assistant is designed to construct complex queries and provide links to the results.
                        
It can also assist in finding information about the OED platform itself. The assistant does not engage in conversation or generate answers independently, but it effectively processes advanced search requests.
                        
Try asking:
                        
                            
Which words in English are borrowed from French?
                            
Which words were first used by Charles Dickens?
                            
How are words added to the dictionary?
                        
                        
For looking up a specific word, it is recommended to use the search bar located at the top of every OED page.
                        
Please note: The number of queries that can be submitted to the assistant per day is limited to no more than 10 requests.
