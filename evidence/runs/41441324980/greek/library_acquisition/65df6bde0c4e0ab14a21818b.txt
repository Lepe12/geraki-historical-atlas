							Περιγραφή
							


								

									

									

										

											
												
												

													THE MAIN AIM OF THIS THESIS IS THE STUDY OF THE TECTONIC FEATURES OF THE  METAMORPHIC BASEMENT OF THE ISLANDS MILOS AND KIMOLOS. THE  MORPHOLOGY OF THAT CRYSTALLINE BASEMENT IS OF PRIMARY INTEREST DUE TO  THE ABNORMAL HEAT FLOW OBSERVED IN BOTH ISLANDS. FURTHERMORE, THE  LOCALIZATION OF AREAS SHOWING VERY SMALL RESISTIVITY VALUES SHOULD  SERVE AS A DIRECT INDICATION OF CIRCULATION OF GEOTHERMAL FLUIDS.  GRAVITY AND ELECTRICAL RESISTIVITY DATA WERE EMPLOYED FOR THE PURPOSE  OF THIS DISSERTATION. ALL DATA HAVE BEEN GIVEN TO THE GEOPHYSICAL  LABORATORY OF THE UNIVERSITY OF THESSALONIKI BY THE P.P.C. (PUBLIC POWER  CORPORATION).THE DATA WERE COMPOSED FROM SEVERAL SETS MEASURED AT  DIFFERENT TIMES BY DIFFERENT FIELD CREWS. THE AUTHOR PARTICIPATED TO  THE FIELD CREW IN THE ACQUISITIONOF A PART OF THE DATA, BASICALLY FOR  KIMOLOS, DURING AUTUMN 1984. (SHORTENED)
