EGU Today comments and
suggestions for improvement
▪

App issues (by far most frequent complaint): not loading
properly/not accessible, only available from Wednesday
onwards, have automatic notification when new issue is available

▪

More advertisement, especially since it's no longer on paper, and
more screens

▪

Some people have suggested we roll back the paper version;
others complimented us on going paperless

▪

Some people have asked us to send the email notification about
the newsletter earlier in the day or the night before

▪

Fix email formatting, make it more clear email is preview only

▪

More extensive, more info, division-specific newsletter (probably
not feasible?)
