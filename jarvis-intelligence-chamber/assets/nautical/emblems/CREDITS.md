`north_african` uses no file: a red field with three white crescents is drawn in code, the period Ottoman/corsair naval flag
(J. Martykán, "A history of Ottoman naval flags", Proc. XVII Int. Congress of Vexillology, 1999; Andrea Vicentino,
*Battle of Lepanto*, Doge's Palace, Venice, c.1595–1605). Evidence specific to Algiers, Tunis and Tripoli in 1577 is thin.
