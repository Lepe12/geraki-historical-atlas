# PCT General Research Doctrine — Source-First Historical Discovery v1.0

## Scope
Universal doctrine inherited by every historical research job. Place-specific projects (including Kythnos) are tests/instances, not architecture.

## Opening research wave: contemporary observers and travellers
Before narrow actor/name chasing, identify and search travel accounts, itineraries, diplomatic journeys, geographical descriptions, portolan-related texts, missionary/consular narratives and other contemporary observers relevant to the target century and region. For Mediterranean work, prioritize Mediterranean and Eastern Mediterranean travellers/observers whose route, residence or information network could cover the target place. Search original-language editions, contemporary translations, manuscript copies and later critical editions, while preserving source genealogy.

## Parallel archival wave
Run archive discovery in parallel, adapted to the target century, political geography and event type. Core families:
- Spanish Monarchy: Archivo General de Simancas and relevant Spanish state/royal/diplomatic/naval holdings.
- Venetian: Archivio di Stato di Venezia and Venetian Senate, bailo/provveditore, maritime, diplomatic and colonial records.
- French: Archives nationales, Archives diplomatiques and relevant naval/consular/provincial holdings.
- English/British: The National Archives and relevant State Papers, Admiralty, diplomatic, consular and merchant records.
- Dutch: Nationaal Archief and relevant VOC/States-General/admiralty/diplomatic/merchant holdings.
- German-speaking repositories: BSB and relevant state/city/ducal/imperial manuscript and printed collections where the target network warrants them.
- Italian states: relevant state archives and libraries (Genoa, Naples/Sicily, Tuscany, Papal States, Savoy and others according to period).
- Ottoman: Ottoman archival/manuscript/chronicle/register traditions appropriate to the century, including imperial, provincial, naval, fiscal and diplomatic records where accessible.
- North African/Barbary: archival, manuscript, chronicle, consular, diplomatic and captivity/ransom records connected with Algiers, Tunis, Tripoli, Morocco and counterpart European archives.

## Period books and printed sources
For the target century, systematically search books, pamphlets, gazettes, chronicles, travel narratives, geographical works, naval/corsair accounts, diplomatic collections and contemporary printed news. Search editions published during the period and near-contemporary retrospective works. Later scholarship is a discovery/citation-chase aid, never a substitute for inspecting the cited original when available.

## Adaptive rule
Repositories and source families are not a fixed checklist. The agent expands/weights them from the target century, place, languages, sovereignty, trade routes, war context and discovered names. It searches historical spelling/toponym variants and multilingual event vocabulary. Actor remains UNKNOWN unless evidence establishes identity.

## Acquisition and verification
Catalogue hit -> locate digital object -> automatically acquire available OCR/full text/IIIF/page derivatives -> EVENT-FIRST search -> exact-page mapping -> download exact scan -> original inspection -> event/date/place extraction -> source genealogy -> independent corroboration -> Verification -> QA.
Catalogue metadata, OCR, later quotations, or acquisition success alone never establish an event.


## Discovery retention and dual-score rule
Scientific filtering controls automation and claim strength; it does not erase research material. Every discovered source, OCR context, unresolved lead, contradiction and off-target candidate remains recoverable in Research Discovery with its triage reason.

Every usable discovery exposes two independent non-probabilistic assessments:
- **Research Value (0–100):** how worthwhile the lead is to inspect, chase or compare.
- **Evidence Confidence (0–100):** how mature, traceable and claim-ready the evidence is.

A lead may therefore have high Research Value and low Evidence Confidence. `EXCLUDE_FROM_ACQUISITION` means only that the system will not automatically spend acquisition resources on it; the record remains visible and preserved. Only an explicit legal/privacy/user removal policy may delete a retained discovery.
