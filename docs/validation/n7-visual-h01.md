The functional preview `/n7-apex-step5/` (branch `preview/n7-apex-step5-guide`, release `2c3286f6ce6b899a`) and production `/n7-apex/` are not written by this branch. Not changed: backend, public chat route, voice code and assets (`useN7Voice.ts`, `N7FemaleVoice.ts`, worker), conversation logic, joint rig, limits, moods, assembly, breathing, shockwave, and the `/avatar-3d/` stock-model trial. This branch's workflow run skips the public-chat and voice deployment steps. `N7Workspace.tsx`, `N7ApexStage.tsx` and `N7HumanoidView.tsx` change only the path check that recognises an isolated preview, so the trial path behaves like the functional one.

## 1. H01 as a second face

The approved eyeless N7 face stays the default. The dock has a face selector (N7 / H01); the choice is remembered in the browser and `?face=h01` selects it directly.

H01 is not a new renderer. `prepare_h01_figure.py` composes the committed, owner-supplied `n7-head-h01.webp` onto the approved body and the existing scene loads that raster instead (`n7-humanoid-h01-v1.webp`, 800×1200, so the detailed face is rendered at 2.5× the line-art resolution). The same neck/skull joints, limits, state postures, moods and voice reaction apply.

- Turning: a realistic face shows any warping, so with H01 the rig uses a flatter face plate (`faceRelief`): eyes, nose and mouth turn as one piece and the curvature moves to the sides under the hair. The first plate was shallow (depth 42) and the owner found the motion reduced; it is now 58 deep with the same flat profile, which restores the travel of the turn without warping the features. The approved N7 face keeps its original volume exactly.
- Proportion: uniform scale so hair top and chin land on the approved head's own points (y 20 and 200 of 480). Face width comes out at 95 units against 90 for the approved face. The face and chin are not warped.
- One neck: the owner twice reported that the neck looked attached rather than continuous. The first approach (narrowing the H01 neck into the body's thin line-art neck and cutting its flare) left small outward hooks at the neck base and an abrupt change of width, and was dropped. Now H01 keeps its own neck at its natural width, unwarped. Its neck flares into the shoulders about 13 units higher than the body's shoulder line, so the body's shoulder lines are raised next to the neck to meet that flare (nothing further out or lower on the body moves). H01 then dissolves softly sideways along the shoulder line and downward over the upper chest, its matte skin first and its bright filaments last. The approved line-art head and its loose strands are removed. With H01 the skin weights let the loose hair beside the neck go earlier, so the raised shoulders stay fixed while the head turns (`local-h01-neck-detail.jpg`, `local-h01-neck-front-right-left.jpg`, `local-h01-neck-down-up.jpg`).
- Light and colour: H01's blue is turned 6° toward N7 cyan and its orange 5° toward N7 gold; the left shoulder lines take a restrained warm key to match H01's warm-left, cool-right lighting.

No image generation was used. Limits: the same as the approved face — only a front view exists, so the turn stays bounded (±0.34 rad) and there is no profile. The figure is screen-blended, so the realistic H01 skin is slightly translucent over bright scenery; the new environment dissolves into dark mist behind the figure for that reason.

## 2. Environment

The owner did not see a change in the first attempt (a regrade of the existing mountain raster, removed again) and chose a newly designed scene. `prepare_backdrop_v3.py` paints `n7-environment-v3.webp` (2240×1260) entirely by code, with no source image and no image generation: a navy sky with a star field and a faint cyan veil, a gold-to-cyan glow on the horizon, six alpine ranges receding in haze with snow that catches the light on the tall side peaks, and mist in the valleys. Behind the figure the ranges dissolve into soft dark mist across the full width of the body, so no ridge line or horizon edge shows through the translucent, screen-blended figure. It is stylised, not photographic.

Living highlights (guide Step 6) are the existing `N7Backdrop.tsx` mechanism, unchanged: one analysis of the loaded image picks up to twelve of its already-bright cyan or warm spots, and a small patch in the image's own colours at each spot fades between 8% and 78% opacity on a 9.6 s cycle with staggered phases. No per-frame analysis; Effects Off and reduced motion hide the layer. On the new scene twelve are found, sitting on the snow-lit peaks.

## 3. Menu

Presentation only; every option keeps its accessible name. One slim glass dock, grouped ΔΙΑΘΕΣΗ (Idle, Listening, Thinking, Speaking) · ΠΡΟΣΩΠΟ (N7, H01) · ΠΡΟΒΟΛΗ (Original, Particles, Compare) · ΣΚΗΝΗ (Assembly/Replay, Effects, Technical details, Πραγματικό 3D, Skip), with the live status at the left. Skip keeps its slot when it does not apply, so neither the dock nor the figure jumps when assembly ends. No blur effects are used (a first version with glass blur raised the software-rendered frame time from 44 to 51 ms and was dropped). The voice panel is restyled by CSS from `N7GuideView.tsx` only (centred card; a single pill when collapsed). Header, dock and voice panel sit outside the figure's box. On phones the dock stays one horizontally scrolling row, as before.

### Update — Greek voice starts sooner (owner: "it does not answer immediately")

Cause, measured on the live trial with the real Greek Piper voice and only the chat reply as a fixture: the text reply appears in about 1 s (the N7 chat route itself answers in about 0.6 s), but the first spoken sound came **51.3 s** after the reply on a first visit and **10.9 s** on a later visit. The voice downloaded about 90 MB only when first asked to speak, loaded the model again on every new page, and synthesised a first piece of up to 200 characters before any sound.

Change (voice code, at the owner's request): 2.5 s after an isolated preview opens, the voice is prepared in the background — downloaded on the first visit (read from the browser cache afterwards), model loaded and one tiny synthesis run. Skipped when the browser asks to save data or reports a 2G connection, and when another voice is selected. The first spoken piece is now the first sentence (at most about 90 characters); later pieces are unchanged. The worker file is now `n7-female-voice-worker-v2.js` so browsers cannot keep the old one. The phonemizer still runs once per piece, exactly as before.

Measured locally after the change (real Piper, real Web Audio): page open 30 s before asking — first sound 0.97 s after the reply on a first visit, 0.87 s on a later visit; asking immediately on a first visit — 4.3 s (local server, so download time is not representative). A 279-character reply was spoken completely, in order, in 3 pieces: 17.8 s of speech over 21.7 s, i.e. about 2 s of silence between pieces while the next one is synthesised. That gap is unchanged by this work. All twelve browser suites pass.

Data cost: on a phone, opening the preview now downloads the 90 MB voice in the background the first time even if the visitor never asks it to speak.

Live after the change — release `630dfb4e17c9daf5` ([run](https://github.com/Lepe12/geraki-historical-atlas/actions/runs/43249741153)), same measurement as before: page open 30 s before asking — first sound **1.5 s** after the reply on a first visit (was 51.3 s) and **0.85 s** on a later visit (was 10.9 s); asking immediately on a first visit — **9.6 s**, while the background download is still running. ## MIRA reference — galaxy assembly, speaking light, menu, smoke environment

The owner supplied a 38-second screen recording of a reference figure ("MIRA") and asked for N7 to reach that level, in N7 cyan/gold with a MIRA-style background, starting with the galaxy assembly, the speaking light and the minimal menu with sounds. What the recording shows: a light core is born, matter swirls out of it as a rotating galaxy, the galaxy stands up into a dotted silhouette, a burst with lightning and a ring reveals the lit figure; a soft vertical light inside the dark face while it talks; occasional small arcs on the body; a dark smoky background with long floating strands; small monospace controls in the corners; sound. Nothing from the recording is copied: no frames, sounds or artwork, only the behaviour, rebuilt here in code for N7's own approved figure.

- **Galaxy assembly** (`N7HeadSurface.ts`): the same sampled artwork grains now start at a heart core (160, 297), leave it in order as a two-armed rotating galaxy of faint stardust, the galaxy grows and stands up to the height of the figure, and each grain settles on its own place. `N7GuideFx.ts` draws the burst on a 2D layer above the figure: a flash at the heart and branching bolts while the figure is revealed; the existing completion ring now starts at the heart. Six seconds as before; Replay, Skip, Effects Off and reduced motion behave as before.
- **Living arcs**: every 5–11 s a short, faint crackle runs along the shoulders or arms for about a quarter of a second. Off with Effects Off and in reduced motion.
- **Speaking light**: while Speaking, a soft vertical light appears inside the dark, featureless face, driven by the real playback level of the Greek voice (a gentle pulse in the manual preview). It lights only the empty face, never the drawn lines; nothing is added to the face. With H01 it is kept to 20% because that face is not dark.
- **Sounds** (`N7GuideFx.ts`): synthesised in the browser, no audio files — a rising hum and shimmer during assembly, a crackle and low thump at the burst, a soft three-note chime at completion. Separate audio context from the voice; audible only after the visitor has interacted with the page (browser rule), so the automatic assembly on first opening is silent. New "Sound On/Off" control.
- **Menu**: small monospace capitals, live status and the four states at the bottom left; face, view and scene controls at the bottom right, on one row at desktop widths. Every option keeps its accessible name.
- **Environment**: `n7-environment-v4.webp`, painted by code: near-black navy with layered N7 cyan/teal smoke and sparse stars, darker behind the figure. Live layers in `N7Backdrop.tsx`: a slow drifting copy of the smoke (CSS) and seven long, faint floating strands (half-resolution canvas, about 30 frames a second). Both are off with Effects Off and in reduced motion, so the Effects Off view is still exactly the static image. The living highlights still sit on the image's own brightest wisps; only 2 qualify on this darker scene (12 on the mountain scene).

Tests changed: `test_complete_guide.cjs` no longer requires a left-to-right build (the old river); it requires the galaxy to stay centred and, when the early frame really is early (progress < 0.40), to stand up (height ×1.12). On this slow software renderer the early frame sometimes arrives late (one of three local runs captured it at 0.49); that run records the skip in its limitations instead of passing silently. `test_face_menu.cjs` checks the new environment file. All twelve suites pass locally. Not done yet from the reference: the stronger glow and larger framing of the figure, and the speed control.

## Owner-approved mood pictures (guide step 1 and 3)

The owner supplied Listening, Thinking and Speaking pictures and two assembly references, and approved using the mood pictures with the alignment shown in `docs/validation/n7-reference-pack/` (uniform scale, a measured 8% vertical squash for Listening and Speaking, a shift; nothing redrawn). `prepare_mood_art.py` produces `n7-mood-{listening,thinking,speaking}-v1.webp` at the idle's 320×480 frame; the builder pins their SHA-256.

With the approved N7 face, each mood now shows its own picture instead of a colour variation computed from the idle. The mechanism is the guide's: the dots never move for a mood change; each dot reads the new picture at its own position inside a circle that grows from the face over about a second (`local-approved-moods-idle-listening-thinking-speaking-bloom.jpg`, last panel mid-bloom). Speaking blends from the idle toward the Speaking picture with the real playback level (32% in silence, 100% when loud), so the face still follows the voice; the extra code-drawn face light is reduced because the picture carries its own. With H01 the computed colour variations remain, since no H01 mood pictures exist. The assembly references are not used as textures (the guide treats them as visual references only).

Local: all twelve browser suites pass; voice reactivity measured 9,790 brighter face pixels loud versus quiet. Known differences inherited from the pictures: some hair strands change between moods, and Listening has no right-hand gold curve in the face.
