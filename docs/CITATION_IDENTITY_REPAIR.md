The repair separates acquisition deduplication from cited-work identity. All substantial query title tokens must appear in a candidate's title for automatic download. Partial variants and rejected search candidates remain retained as discovery leads. Exact title coverage still does not establish author, edition or the identity of downloaded bytes. Acquired candidates receive an explicit `CITATION_IDENTITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED` hold, including legacy receipts; they are not downloaded again just to restate the hold. Exhausted search attempts also remain unresolved.

The acceptance validator independently reconstructs identity holds from acquisition receipts, so an old empty unresolved list cannot produce overall success. It reports query, receipt path and candidate URLs under `citation_identity_holds`, the count under `citation_identity_review_queries`, and a `CITATION_IDENTITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED` blocker. Command Center already consumes the validation blockers and remains PARTIAL for an incomplete result. No historical verification gate is relaxed.
