Visual inspection of three pages of the actual stored Koutzakiotis (2023) PDF confirmed that the selected passages match the document: scan 0 is the English abstract, scan 3 is printed page 221, and scan 8 is printed page 226. The PDF SHA-256 matches the acquisition receipt. These are inspected pages of a modern secondary study, not inspected seventeenth-century originals. The precise inspection scope and source-genealogy leads are recorded in [audits/stored-page-inspection.json](audits/stored-page-inspection.json).

One critical chain led to Hacke (1699), page 42, and the chapter containing Roberts's account. The current Greek citation receipt had exhausted searches for the chapter title without acquiring it. The acquired bibliography also names the containing book, *A Collection of Original Voyages*, but the reference parser did not emit that unquoted container as a separate query. It now preserves both the quoted chapter and the containing book when an explicit edited-volume citation supplies them. The same rule supports Greek `στο:` and English `in:` and does not depend on this place, person or title.

The inspected pages also give Piacenza (1688), page 239; Roux (1764), plate 109; and ANF Marine 3 JJ 219, no.1, page 105. These are retained primary-source leads. None of these leads is promoted to VERIFIED, and multiple mentions within the same secondary study are not independent corroboration.
